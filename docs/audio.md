# Audio

Music design v3 (2026-10-22, scheduled run 17). Ambience, tension layer and argument voices come from design v2
(written the same day from the main chat).

All sound is rendered offline by the project's own synthesizer (`scripts/audio/synth.gd`) into 16-bit WAV
(`tools/build_audio.gd`) and loaded at runtime by `WavLoader`. Nothing is downloaded; everything is deterministic
(fixed seeds).

## Tension layer

`layer_tension` (32 s loop, atonal so it sits under any track: sub-rumble, beating inharmonic metal partials,
60 bpm muffled pulse, faint high tone) plays on the Music bus. Level = max of
- selected character's stress (`smoothstep(45, 90, stress) × 0.6`),
- a nearby argument (`conflict_env`, 1 at the argument, scaled by distance to the selected character up to 16 m,
  half-life 14 s),
- anxiety from PsycheFX (`psyche_level × 0.5`).

It rises fast (0.6/s) and fades slowly (0.12/s). Music is ducked at full tension (the test measures about −10 dB on
the Music bus including its base level).

## Arguments you can hear

`social_event(sim, positive=false, …)` (argument or insult; emitted for both participants) triggers:
- a raised **voice** at the character's head as `AudioStreamPlayer3D` – formant synthesis of Czech vowels and noise
  consonants, unintelligible by design (no words); 4 clips (`vox_argue_a/b`, `vox_argue_rough_a/b`);
- pitch from `CharacterData.voice.pitch` (−1..1 → ±~5 semitones), rough variant when `voice.roughness > 0.45`;
  **never** derived from grammatical gender or name;
- distance attenuation with a low-pass ("through the wall");
- the second participant cuts in 0.35–0.9 s later; per-character cooldown 5 s; max 3 voices at once; no voices at
  game speed ≥ 3 (tension still rises);
- a **door slam** 3.5–6 s later near the argument, with a chance from the character's stress and temperament
  (`slam_chance`, 5–75 %, see Audio Engine sound set).

Purely cosmetic and local to each peer: no effect on simulation or network.

## Audio Engine sound set (27 October 2026)

Rendered with the **Audio Engine** connector (Surge XT synthesizer, its built-in synth and templates), converted
to mono 22 050 Hz 16-bit and stored in `res://audio/engine/`. Unlike `audio/music` and `audio/sfx` these files
cannot be regenerated by `tools/build_audio.gd`, so they are committed to git (`.gitignore` exception
`!audio/engine/*.wav`). Playback lives in `scripts/audio/engine_sfx.gd` (child node `EngineSfx` of AudioManager).
The five existing music tracks are unchanged.

| File(s) | Use |
|---|---|
| `step_wood_1..4` | footsteps inside the house (floor 12.4 × 9.4 m around the origin) |
| `step_grass_1..4` | footsteps outside (garden, path) |
| `loop_pages` | loop while reading (`read`) |
| `loop_phone` | soft phone taps while texting or doomscrolling (`text_friend`, `doomscroll`) |
| `loop_pen` | pen on paper while journaling (`journal`) |
| `sfx_fridge`, `sfx_bottle` | fridge door for `snack`; fridge + bottle opening for `drink` |
| `loop_duvet` | one quiet duvet rustle when lying down (`sleep`, `nap`, `hide`) |
| `amb_clock` | clock ticking at night (Ambience bus, weight up to 0.32 between about 22:00 and 06:00) |
| `music_creator` | character creator/editor theme: A minor, 66 BPM, bells (Surge "Smooth Bells"), dark pad ("Subtle Pad"), sub bass ("Sub 1"), no drums – written in the spirit of the night track |

- **Footsteps** follow each character's real horizontal speed (stride 0.7 m → interval 0.2–0.9 s), with a random
  variant, small pitch/volume variation, nothing while standing, after teleports, at game speed ≥ 3 or farther than
  22 m from the camera. Works for local characters and network puppets (position-based).
- **Action sounds** are 3D at the character, fade in/out and stop when the action ends; actions that already have
  an object sound (stove, shower, TV, treadmill) are not doubled.
- **Creator/editor music**: while the creator or editor panel is open the music director switches to `creator`
  immediately (no 12 s minimum) and back when it closes.
- **Door slam by stress and temperament** (closes the open item): `AudioManager.slam_chance(sim)` =
  0.12 + 0.38 × smoothstep(35, 90, stress) + 0.25 Hot-headed + 0.12 Impulsive + 0.08 Grumpy − 0.06 Loner,
  clamped to 0.05–0.75; without data the old 30 %.
- The MIDI source and the stereo master are also saved in the Audio Engine library project `lifesim`
  (`midi/creator_theme.mid`, `sounds/creator_theme.wav`).

## Character voices in ordinary conversations (27 October 2026)

Successful, non-argument conversations (`SimAgent.conversation` with `success` and not `argued`) now make both
participants speak a short wordless line in **their own voice** (`VoiceSynth`, see
[character-creator.md](character-creator.md#voice-27-october-2026-scheduled-run)): style cheerful when the average
conversation mood is ≥ 25, sad when ≤ −25, otherwise calm. Lines are 1.3 s at 16 kHz, synthesised in
`WorkerThreadPool` (main thread only polls finished tasks), cached in a 24-entry LRU (3 sentence variants per voice),
played as `AudioStreamPlayer3D` at the head (unit size 3, max distance 28 m, attenuation filter). Limits: 9 s
cooldown per character, only within 12 m of the selected character, no lines at the fastest speed, at most 4 pending
syntheses and the shared limit of 3 voices. Argument voices keep their pre-rendered files. Everything is local
cosmetics on each peer; nothing is sent over the network.

## Crying vocal cue (28 October 2026)

When a character **starts** crying (`SimAgent.crying_changed`, an edge event driven by the emotional model's
hysteresis: sadness ≥ 70 starts, ≤ 55 stops), AudioManager plays one short synthetic sad vocal cue in that character's
own voice through the same asynchronous VoiceSynth pipeline (`smutne` style). It is a cue, not realistic sobbing.
Limits: offline only (a connected host or client never voices private emotions), 35 s real-time cooldown per
character, hearing distance and the shared 3-voice cap, nothing at speed ≥ 3, and queued cue renders are dropped
after a speed-up or more than 4 s of backlog. Restoring a save never fakes a new onset. Tests: `test_voice`
(99 checks) and `test_emotional_state` (55).

## Czech text-to-speech: characters speak their speech-bubble lines (28 October 2026)

Characters can now **say the actual line shown in their speech bubble** (the public `speech` text) in their own voice.
Everything is self-contained: no network, no external engine, no recorded speech.

- **`scripts/audio/czech_g2p.gd`** – rule-based Czech grapheme-to-phoneme conversion. Normalises the text (lower case,
  NFD → composed letters, foreign letters folded, emoji/symbols/control characters dropped, length cap 200 characters,
  digits spelled as Czech number words 0–9999, longer digit runs read digit by digit). Rules: ch → x; y/ý → i/í;
  ů → ú; w → v, q → kv, x → ks; bě/pě/vě/fě → bje…, mě → mňe, dě/tě/ně → ďe/ťe/ňe; di/ti/ni/dí/tí/ní → ďi/ťi/ňi
  (dy/ty/ny stay hard); ř (voiced trill-fricative, devoiced after/before voiceless and finally: *tři* [tř̊i]); long
  vowels á é í ó ú; diphthongs ou (always), au (word-initially), eu (not in the ne- prefix); dž; n before k/g → ŋ;
  regressive voicing assimilation in obstruent clusters, also across word boundaries inside a phrase (*kde* [gde],
  *led byl* [led bil]); v is a target but never a trigger (*tvůj* stays [tvúj]); v → f before voiceless (*všechno*
  [fšexno]); sh → sx; final devoicing before a pause, vowel or sonorant (*led* [let], *obchod* [opxot]); one-consonant
  prepositions k s v z are glued to the next word (*v lese* [vlese], *v pokoji* [fpokoji]). Syllables use vowel nuclei
  plus syllabic r / l (*vlk*, *krk*) and final m (*sedm*).
- **Prosody** – fixed stress on the first syllable of every stress unit: monosyllabic prepositions take the stress of
  the following word (*NA stole*), enclitics (se, si, je, jsem, by, mi, ti, …) are unstressed. Phrases come from
  punctuation: statement (`.`) final fall; yes/no question (`?` without a question word) low last stressed syllable and
  a rise to the end; wh-question (kdo, co, kde, kdy, proč, jak, kam, odkud, kolik, který/á/é, čí, …) high start and
  falling end; exclamation (`!`) 1.5× range and stronger accents; comma/semicolon/colon slight rise and a short pause;
  ellipsis trailing low with fade. F0 declines across the utterance; the contour is kept inside −10…+8 semitones
  around the speaker's own base and 50–420 Hz by linear compression (no clipped plateaus). Micro-prosody (v2): cosine
  interpolation + 15 ms zero-phase smoothing, deterministic two-component drift (±0.45 st / 120 ms and ±0.18 st /
  55 ms), intrinsic vowel pitch (i/u +0.3 st, a −0.25 st), +0.7 st after voiceless and −0.45 st after voiced
  obstruents decaying over ~35 ms, accent peaks slightly after the nucleus onset, the yes/no rise spread over the
  last nucleus (≤ ~80 st/s). Durations: short vowel 85 ms (unstressed ×0.96, stressed ×1.06), long vowels 1.72×,
  diphthongs 165 ms, phrase-final lengthening ×1.45 (×1.9 in a trailing ellipsis), all divided by the speaking rate
  (voice.tempo × style); pauses comma 200 ms, period/question 420 ms (× style).
- **Synthesis (`scripts/audio/czech_tts.gd`, v2 since 29 Oct 2026)** – see *Natural voice rework* below. KLGLOTT88
  glottal flow derivative (style open quotient 0.46–0.74, polyBLEP closure, extra one-pole tilt), Gaussian jitter
  σ 0.38 % + 1.6 %·roughness² and shimmer σ 2.5 % + 7.5 %·roughness², a mild alternate-period creak only above
  roughness 0.6, glottal-pulsed aspiration noise; Klatt cascade of six unity-gain resonators (F1–F4 from the phoneme,
  fixed F5 4.5 kHz and F6 5.6 kHz scaled by the tract; bandwidths 70/95/150/220/300/500 Hz, F1 widened with F0);
  nasal antiformant (m 1.0, n 1.75, ň 2.35, ŋ 2.9 kHz) + murmur low-pass; frication as 4th-order band-limited noise
  (s ≈ 5.6 kHz, š ≈ 3.1 kHz, f/v broad and weak, x ≈ 1.9 kHz; voiced z ž v pulsed by the glottis), h as breathy
  aspiration through the next vowel's formants, plosives as closure (voice bar for b d ď g) + place-shaped 3.5 ms
  decaying burst (velar burst follows the next vowel) + 14 ms aspiration/voice-onset delay after voiceless stops,
  affricates c č dz dž, l, j, r as one tap (two for syllabic/long r), ř as a ~26 Hz trill with friction in the
  openings. All parameters are control tracks (one value per 16 samples) smoothed zero-phase: formants τ 9 ms
  (≈ 40 ms transitions, natural undershoot in fast/short syllables, consonant F2 values act as loci), voicing τ 3.5 ms,
  noise τ 2.2 ms. Syllable gain: stressed ×1.12, unstressed ×0.94, −16 % across a phrase, phrase-final nucleus fades
  by up to 50 % with extra breath, phrase-initial vowels start softly. Output: DC block, 7 kHz Butterworth low-pass,
  six early reflections (7–46 ms, ≤ −20 dB, damped), 2:1 compression above −10 dB, peak −3 dBFS for the loudest
  style (calm ≈ −3.7 dBFS, sad ≈ −4.6 dBFS), 12 ms fades. `voice.pitch` sets the base F0 (≈ 77–331 Hz),
  `voice.timbre` the tract scale (±12 %) and source brightness, `voice.roughness` jitter/shimmer/noise/creak;
  styles klidně (soft, warm), vesele (higher, livelier, brighter), naštvaně (tenser, pressed, louder, never above the
  F0 ceiling), smutně / unaveně (slower, breathier, lower range), šeptem (whisper). Nothing is derived from gender,
  identity, grammatical gender, name or body. Same input → identical bytes (own FNV-1a hash and seeded RNG).
- **API** – `VoiceSynth.speak(text, voice, style, sr) -> AudioStreamWAV`, `speak_buffer`, `tts_phonemes`,
  `tts_transcription`, `tts_plan` (segments, syllables with stress, phrases, F0 every 5 ms), `tts_f0_contour`,
  `tts_cache_key`, `tts_style_for_emotion`.
- **In game** – `SimAgent.say()` and `SimPuppet.net_speech` emit `speech_started(sim, text)`. In the **Řeč z textu**
  mode AudioManager speaks the line (after the profanity filter, exactly as displayed) with the speaker's public
  `look.voice` and a style from the **displayed** emotion (naštvaný → angry, smutný → sad, unavený → tired, šťastný →
  cheerful, otherwise calm). Synthesis runs in `WorkerThreadPool` at 16 kHz into the shared 24-entry LRU cache; 2.5 s
  real-time cooldown per character, the 12 m hearing distance and 3-voice cap of conversation lines, at most 4 pending
  jobs, nothing at speed ≥ 3 and jobs older than 4 s or overtaken by a speed-up are dropped. Arguments keep the
  recorded `vox_argue_*` takes: a line whose speaker has just started an argument voice is not spoken as well (raised
  voices from formant TTS were not judged convincing). In multiplayer every peer synthesises locally from public data
  only (bubble text, look.voice, net_emotion); no private state is read and nothing extra is sent.
- **Setting** – `GameSettings.character_voices` (persisted in `user://settings.cfg`), menu **Hlasy postav: Beze slov /
  Řeč z textu / Vypnuto**, default **Beze slov** since 29 Oct 2026 (the owner found the first TTS voice robotic and
  creepy; the default returns to Řeč z textu only after the owner approves the reworked voice). *Beze slov* keeps the previous wordless conversation lines and no
  TTS; *Vypnuto* silences conversation lines, TTS, the crying cue and argument voices (door slams and the tension layer
  stay).
- **Creator / editor** – the Hlas section has a **Zkusit větu** field (default "Ahoj, jak se máš? Dneska je hezky."),
  **▶ Zkusit větu** and **Otázka** ("Půjdeš se mnou zítra ven?") to hear the draft voice speak; rendered off the main
  thread, cached with the other previews.
- **Quality and limits (honest)** – see the rework section: all claims are measurements, nobody involved in the
  rework could listen. Intelligibility has **not** been verified by a listener or a speech recogniser (no ASR model
  could be downloaded: the session's network allowed package registries only). It is still a compact rule-based
  formant synthesiser: clearly synthetic, prosody is rule-based, unusual words, loanwords, abbreviations, prefix
  boundaries (po-učit vs. pouze) and homographs may be mispronounced.

### Natural voice rework (29 October 2026)

The owner called the first version "robotic and creepy" and chose to improve the existing synthesis (no neural model,
no Simlish). Diagnosis on the 8 owner samples + 3 extra sentences (Praat/parselmouth, numpy/scipy; scripts kept out of
the repo) found:

1. **Muffled, "in-head" spectrum** – parallel formants with fixed relative gains and a 2 kHz source low-pass: the
   2–4 kHz band of vowels was −36 dB re 0–1 kHz (natural ≈ −20…−26), almost nothing above 4.5 kHz.
2. **Hoarse/creepy source** – uniform white jitter ≈ 1 % and shimmer ≈ 6 % per period, period-doubling
   "subharmonics" from roughness 0.35 on (the 0.5-roughness sample growled), almost no breath noise in modal voice.
3. **Robotic pitch** – piecewise-linear F0 with flat plateaus (55 % of 30 ms vowel windows < 0.1 st movement, flat
   runs up to 115 ms, hard clamps at −10/+8 st that pinned the angry/high samples flat).
4. **Choppy envelope** – one constant amplitude per phoneme switched with 5 ms ramps (5 ms level jumps p99 20 dB).
5. **Clicks** – audible transients at the utterance onset, at fricative/sonorant joins and a formant jump at every
   plosive (8 isolated broadband transients in the 11 samples).
6. **tanh saturation** on every utterance and a completely dry signal.

| Measurement (mean of 11 sentences, 22.05 kHz) | before | after | natural target |
|---|---|---|---|
| vowel 2–4 kHz band re 0–1 kHz | −36.1 dB | −22.1 dB | ≈ −20…−26 dB |
| vowel energy above 4 kHz re 0–1 kHz | −37.8 dB | −40.1 dB (with a smooth roll-off instead of a cliff) | — |
| vowel HNR (Praat cc) | 21.3 dB | 18.2 dB (calm ≈ 19–20, sad/tired ≈ 13–14) | modal ≈ 17–22, breathy lower |
| jitter / shimmer (Praat local, connected speech) | 1.46 % / 9.0 % | 1.16 % / 9.3 % | low (shimmer includes intended envelope) |
| F0 flat 30 ms windows in vowels (planned contour) | 55 % | 30 % | — |
| longest perfectly flat F0 run in a vowel | 115 ms | 40 ms | none |
| median F0 velocity in vowels | 3.9 st/s | 7.4 st/s | lively speech moves continuously |
| isolated clicks (detector, 11 samples, 22.05 / 16 kHz) | 8 / 2 | 0 / 0 | 0 |
| 5 ms level jump p99 | 20.2 dB | 8.8 dB | — |
| median vowel (short and long) / consonant | 89 / 59 ms | 104 / 59 ms | short 70–110 / 50–100 ms |
| peak | −2.0 dBFS (saturated) | −3.7 dBFS (−3.0 angry) | ≈ −3 dBFS |
| render time, longest sample (2.5 s) | 82 ms | ≈ 100–130 ms | ≪ 1 s |

Per-segment levels re vowels after the rework: j −5, l −6, r −7, n −7, ň −7, m −9, h −9, v −9, z −10, s −11.5,
š −12.4, ž −12.6 dB.
