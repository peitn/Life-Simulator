# Vision & content policy

## The pitch

A life simulation for adults: deep, believable systems instead of a sanitised dollhouse. Choices have
lasting consequences, characters have flaws and histories, and a person's inner life, including mental
illness, is simulated with the same care as hunger or sleep.

## Design pillars

1. **Simulation depth over scripted drama.** Stories emerge from systems: needs, mood, traits, memory,
   relationships and mental state. Hand-written scenes are the exception.
2. **Raw but honest tone.** Arguments, break-ups, infidelity, jealousy, debt and poverty, losing a job,
   addiction (alcohol, gambling) with real consequences, petty crime, gossip and intrigue, illness and
   death, black humour and swearing in dialogue.
3. **Mental health as a core system.** It is not a label or a debuff. Conditions have triggers, good and bad
   days, relapses, healthy and unhealthy coping, support from others and stigma. The path to recovery is
   never closed.
4. **Player agency is sacred.** Symptoms can be involuntary, but the player is never locked out. No single
   choice is always optimal.
5. **Respectful diversity.** Body, gender identity, pronouns, sexual orientation, voice, skin and clothing
   are independent axes. There are no "race" presets and no stereotyped archetypes.
6. **Multiplayer-first.** An authoritative server, with private inner state that only its owner sees.

## Content rules (non-negotiable)

These rules are part of the design and are enforced by tests and audits.

- **Intimacy** is shown only by implication (fade-to-black). There is no explicit content.
- **Minors** never appear in adult themes. Player characters and all characters in adult storylines are 18+.
- **Violence** against real-world groups is never glorified.
- **Self-harm and eating disorders** may only be portrayed through emotions, relationships and
  consequences:
  - no graphic depiction and no descriptions of methods;
  - no playable action that performs self-harm;
  - no numbers such as weight or calories as goals;
  - nothing the player could "optimise", and nothing the game rewards.
  These topics are **hidden by default** (`sh_ed_topics = false`).
- **Suicide is not a playable action.**
- **Personality disorders and other diagnoses are never villain templates.**
- **Content settings.** Every sensitive system respects them: mental-health themes can be shown fully,
  muted or switched off, and the same goes for intrusive thoughts, perceptual distortion effects,
  addiction and profanity.
- **Crisis help** is always reachable from the menu: 116 123 (Czech Republic), 112/155 for emergencies,
  and [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com) internationally. A content warning is shown on
  first launch.

## Planned depth (agreed with reviewers)

- Mental health grows **one axis per development run**, starting from a shared set of interpretable
  dimensions. The following steps add social anxiety and fear of rejection, burnout and anxious
  depression, OCD, ADHD/ADD, bipolar disorder (with hypomania clearly separated from ADHD
  hyperactivity) and autistic traits as profile axes. A diagnosis is a predisposition, never a script.
  Two characters with the same label must behave measurably differently.
- **Emotional presentation**: posture, facial expression, breathing and micro-actions, plus adaptive music
  layers with silence as a first-class state, anti-repetition rules and a "relief" motif. Players should
  feel what a character goes through, not just read a number.
- **Identity in context**: several worldview axes (with no real-world parties and no "good/bad opinion"
  scoring) and a model of who knows what about whom. Coming out is an optional, contextual process.
  Discrimination is never inevitable, and LGBT+ lives include joy, not only conflict.
