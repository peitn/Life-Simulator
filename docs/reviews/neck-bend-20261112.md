# Neck under a lateral bend and the "collar" under body morphs (12 November 2026)

Run `main-claude-1794468128-338000`. Goal: the last two failures of `test_neck_seam` (54/56), open since the
30 October realism pass.

## Findings

1. **Flipped triangles under a lateral bend.** Only one of the 16 test combinations failed: neck length −1,
   body fat 0.71, narrow chest, lateral bend −20°. Seven triangles on the side of the neck (x ≈ −0.05 m,
   y 1.552–1.564 m) turned over. The skin weights were not the cause. The neck-length ramp was: at the side it
   started 2 cm above the body's seam loop (≈ 1.545 m) and ended at 1.588 m (a linear blend in cos θ between the
   chin, 1.556, and the base of the skull, 1.62). A 15 mm shift over about 4 cm with a smoothstep compresses the
   side of the neck by up to ~60 % at −1. Fat and muscle also raise the lower rows of the band by 5–8 mm, and the
   bend compresses the inner side further.
   A horizontal ray profile r(y, θ) of the head mesh showed that the angle of the jaw sits at about
   1.62–1.63 m at the side (1.59–1.61 m at 60°), so the ramp can be much longer there without stretching the jaw.
   A wider in-memory check also found 44 flipped triangles **at rest** for maximum fat + maximum muscle + neck −1,
   a case the test did not cover.
2. **Collar.** The growth of the neck radius from body morphs fell by 2.9 mm per 5 mm of height just below the
   jaw at 60°/300°. About 0.8 mm of that comes from the vertical lift of the neck base (fat and muscle raise the
   body's neck loop) fading out below the jaw. The rest is the body-morph band (8 cm) ending too steeply.

## Known limits

  30 triangles under the chin (a "double chin" a mesh without self-contact cannot model). Realistic bodies pass.
  Possible fix later: a corrective shape `b_neck_len × fat_hi` or a pose corrective driven by the neck bone.
- Checks use CPU linear-blend skinning with the neck and head bones rotating together around the neck joint.
