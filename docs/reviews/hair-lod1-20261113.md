# Hair strand cards for crowd LOD tier 1 (13 Nov 2026)

Run `main-claude-1794597706-359022`, character creator / crowd LOD step 3.

## Goal
Characters between ~5.5 % and 20 % of the screen height (crowd LOD tier 1) still rendered the full strand-card
hair (11–40 k triangles per hairstyle). Individual cards are not readable at that size, but silhouette and coverage
are. Step 3 adds a per-hairstyle LOD1 mesh that `SimVisual` shows in tier 1. Tier 0 keeps the full cards, tier 2 the
proxy envelope from step 2. Purely client-side presentation; nothing is replicated.

## How LOD1 is built (`scripts/assets/hair_rig.gd`, `lod1_pack`)
LOD1 is derived from the finished card arrays (`_arrays_from`) in the same `WorkerThreadPool` task that builds the
cards and the proxy, so skin binding, bone weights and transferred head/body blend shapes are exactly those of the
full cards. Cache key `scalp_lod1:<style>`; `null` means no LOD1 (tier 1 then uses the full cards).

1. **Cards (kind 0)** – a share of whole cards is kept per layer (`CUSTOM0.z`: inner 25 % … outer 60 %), decided by
   the per-strand random value (`CUSTOM0.x`, constant over a card). Kept cards are widened by `1/√share` (max 1.5×)
   around the strand axis, in the rest pose and in every blend-shape target, so coverage stays similar without
   changing the material.
2. **Outline protection** – card segments within 2 mm of the farthest card vertex of their cell are always kept
   (not widened). Two grids: spherical around the head centre (48 × 32; crown, fringe) and cylindrical around the
   vertical head axis (48 azimuths × 2 cm heights; horizontal reach of hanging hair = outline from front, side and top).
   Without it, random thinning tore the outline of long hair (hair-only IoU 0.91–0.93 from the side/top).
3. **Afro shells (kind 4)** – the outermost shell and every second shell inwards.
4. **Other surfaces** (scalp shell, braids, dreads, bun, hair tie) – meshoptimizer via `ImporterMesh.generate_lods`
   on a locally compacted subset, one kind at a time. The coarsest level is used that keeps ≥ 40 % of the indices,
   has an error ≤ 4 mm (`get_surface_lod_size`; ~½ pixel at the near tier-1 boundary at 1080p) and changes ≤ 1 % of
   the welded border edges (the hairline is not a seam to another mesh). Measured errors of the first level: scalp
   shell 0.23 mm, afro 0.94 mm, cornrows 0.95 mm, dreads 6 mm, box braids 13.8 mm – braids and dreads therefore
   stay unsimplified.
5. If fewer than 20 % of the triangles would be saved, no LOD1 is created (`build_stats` records `skip`). This is
   the case for box braids and dreads.
6. The result is compacted: only used vertices, in ascending original order (deterministic); same blend-shape names
   in the same order and the same surface format as the full cards.

## SimVisual
`_apply_model_variant` remembers the full scalp mesh and style; `set_lod_tier` calls `_apply_scalp_lod`, which swaps
full ↔ LOD1 (from the cache only, never blocking) and immediately rewrites the face/body shape weights. Expression
weights are rewritten on the next expression update (tier 1 updates at 10 Hz). In tier 2 the scalp node is hidden
and its mesh is not swapped.

## Review
Gemini (3.5-flash) review – accepted: partner lookup of card vertex pairs now also requires the same card attributes
(robustness if the builder changes). Rejected with evidence: "first-vertex bias / widen overwrite" (cards have
constant attributes and own vertices; the width test matches every pair), "absolute blend targets detach strands"
(the widened morphed card is the intended result; verified on the strongest key), "ArrayMesh RIDs created in worker
threads" (`mesh_from_pack` runs in `_store` on the main thread). Open: thread safety of `ImporterMesh.generate_lods`
inside `WorkerThreadPool` is not independently proven (async tests pass); expression weights on the hair can lag up
to one 10 Hz expression tick after a swap.

## Known limits / next
- Braids and dreads have no LOD1 (tube simplification flattens them); a tube-aware LOD (fewer ring sides) is open.
- Brows and beard cards have no LOD1 yet; procedural parts (hands, feet, torso details) have no LODs.
- No GPU frame-time measurement yet (headless only); visual check via `tools/render_hair_lod1.tscn`.
